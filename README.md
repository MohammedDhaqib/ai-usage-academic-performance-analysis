# ai-usage-academic-performance-analysis
Statistical analysis of AI tool usage and academic performance among students.

Overview
This project investigates the relationship between AI tool usage, AI dependency, and academic performance among college students.

Objectives
- Analyze the extent of AI tool usage among students.
- Examine the relationship between AI usage and academic performance.
- Explore perceived academic benefits and risks.
- Study concerns related to over-dependence and academic integrity.
- Recommend responsible and ethical AI usage.

Methodology
The study uses questionnaire-based survey data and statistical techniques, including descriptive statistics, graphical analysis, correlation analysis, and factor analysis where supported by the available data.

Key Finding

The original project reports a correlation of -0.11 between AI dependency and academic performance. This result requires verification against the underlying data and original calculation procedure.

Limitations
The interpretation is limited by the available sample information, self-reported measures, and the completeness of the original statistical outputs.

Academic Context
B.Sc. Statistics mini-project, Department of Statistics, Loyola College, Chennai.

Note
This repository documents an academic research project. Findings should be interpreted in the context of the study design and available evidence.
